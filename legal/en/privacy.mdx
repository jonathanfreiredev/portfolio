This is the privacy policy for jonathanfreire.com. I take your privacy seriously and want to be clear about what happens with any information you share when you visit the site or get in touch with me.

## Who I am

I am Jonathan Freire, a sole trader (Einzelunternehmer) based in Germany. I work as a Digital Product Architect, helping ambitious companies with strategy, software engineering, and AI integration. I run the business on my own, with no team and no employees.

The "data controller" for this website is me. You can reach me about anything in this policy at [jonathan.freireespinoza@gmail.com](mailto:jonathan.freireespinoza@gmail.com).

## What I collect

I keep this short because I do not collect much.

When you use the contact form, I receive:

- your name
- your email address
- the message you write

There is no newsletter signup, no user account, no comment section, and no payment processing on this site.

When you visit the site, my hosting provider (Framer) automatically processes some technical data so the website can work. This typically includes your IP address, browser type, device type, the page you requested, and the time of the request. Framer uses this data to deliver the site and keep it secure.

I do not knowingly collect any special categories of personal data (such as health information, political opinions, or religious beliefs).

## How I use it

I use the information you give me through the contact form for one purpose: to read your message and reply to you. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR (steps taken at your request before entering into a contract) or, where no project follows, Article 6(1)(f) (your consent when sending the message).

I keep contact form submissions in my email inbox while we are in conversation, and for up to 12 months afterwards in case you come back with a follow-up. If we end up working together, the project records are kept for as long as German tax law requires, typically 10 years.

I use the technical data processed by Framer to operate the website, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot problems. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR, my legitimate interest in running a secure and functional website. Framer retains server logs only as long as needed for this purpose, in line with their own policy.

I do not sell your data. I do not share it for marketing. I do not use it to build advertising profiles about you.

## Cookies and analytics

Framer may set a small number of cookies that are required for the site to work, for example to remember your language or session state. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, and the site will still work.

I currently use Framer Insights to understand, in aggregate, which pages get visited and where visitors come from. This is provided by Framer as part of the hosting platform. I do not use it to identify you personally, and Framer does not give me access to your IP address or any individual identifiers.

At some point in the future I may add an analytics tool. If I do, I will update this section to explain what is collected, on what legal basis, and how you can opt out. I will not turn on any new analytics tool without telling you first.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have a number of rights when it comes to your personal data. In short, you can:

- ask me what I hold about you and get a copy (Article 15)
- have me correct anything that is wrong (Article 16)
- ask me to delete your data (Article 17)
- ask me to pause processing in certain situations (Article 18)
- receive your data in a portable format (Article 20)
- object to processing based on legitimate interest (Article 21)
- withdraw consent at any time, if processing is based on consent (Article 7(3))

To use any of these rights, just email me at [jonathan.freireespinoza@gmail.com](mailto:jonathan.freireespinoza@gmail.com). I will respond within 30 days, as required by law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority responsible for your region. In Germany, the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection (BfDI) maintains a directory of regional authorities at bfd.bund.de, where you can find the one that applies to you.

## Data security

Your data is processed on Framer's infrastructure, which is hosted in the European Union. Framer applies industry standard security measures including encryption in transit and access controls on their side.

On my side, I keep contact form submissions in a private email inbox and only access them to reply to you. I do not share inbox access with anyone.

No system is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute protection. If something does go wrong, I will tell you and notify the relevant authority as required by law.

I may update this policy from time to time, for example if I add a new feature, change a tool, or respond to new legal requirements. The current version is always the one on this page, with the "last updated" date at the top. If a change is significant, I will make a reasonable effort to flag it.
